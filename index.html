About me

I am a fourth-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Southern California, where I am part of the CS Theory Group and the ODDS Group. I am advised by Meisam Razaviyayn and Vatsal Sharan.

I am broadly interested in fundamental problems in optimization and differential privacy.

I am currently looking for research internships for Summer 2027! If you have any open positions and feel that my profile fits your vacancy, please do reach out!
